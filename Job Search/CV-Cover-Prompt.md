# CV & Cover Letter Prompt

A reusable prompt for tailoring your CV and cover letter with an AI assistant. Paste it in along with your CV/cover source (written for LaTeX, but it works for any format) and the job note for the role.

```
Below is my CV and Cover in LaTeX. As well as information for a job listing and other information about the company I'd like to apply for. 

Without changing ANY formatting/LaTeX Only the English Language use. Adjust the Two files to fit the job listing. Prioritise skills mentioned in the job listing and the skills that I have strong evidence for within my CV. As well as any variables relevant such as the role / company name. Do not reorganise my CV keep it chronological. Return A result written entirely in the same format provided, do not add any newline spaces, just cut and paste text. Blank new lines will cause the code to not compile. Adjusted with this new information… Try to keep the CV to two pages and the cover to one page.

Finally add any notes to the end of your message. About design choices and decisions you've made or that I should consider as i review it.
```
