# Job Tracker

The dashboard for your search. Every application is its own note (created from the [[Templates/Job Application|Job Application template]]), and everything below is generated live from those notes' properties. Update `status`, `date_applied` and so on in the note itself and this page follows.

> **Status values:** `not-applied` · `applied` · `interview` · `offer` · `rejected` · `declined`
> The **Stage** column groups these into kanban columns. Rejected/declined cards drop off the board after 14 days untouched.
