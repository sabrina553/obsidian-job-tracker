# Email & Message Templates

Fill in the `[brackets]`. Keep the tone professional and specific — name the role, not just "a job".

## Recruiter — Initial Outreach

> Use straight after applying to a role they posted, or when introducing yourself to a new agency.

```
Subject: [Your Name] — [Role Title] Application / Introduction

Hi [Recruiter Name],

I've just applied for the [Role Title] role at [Company] ([link if available]), and wanted to introduce myself directly.

I'm a [your one-line framing, e.g. software engineer with 3 years' experience] with hands-on experience in [1–2 relevant skills]. I'm currently looking for [type of] roles in [location/remote], and would really value being on your radar for similar opportunities.

I've attached my CV for reference. Would you be able to let me know if you have a moment to set up a quick call?

Thanks for your time,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email] | [LinkedIn]
```

## Recruiter — Weekly Follow-Up

> Send once a week to anyone who hasn't replied. Stop after 2 weeks of silence (see cadence rule in [[Recruiters]]).

```
Subject: Re: [Role Title] Application / Introduction

Hi [Recruiter Name],

Just following up on my note from [date] — I'm still very interested in [Role Title] / similar roles, and wanted to check whether there's any update or a good time to talk this week.

Thanks again,
[Your Name]
```

## Application — Post-Apply Follow-Up

> Use ~1 week after applying directly to a company with no recruiter involved, if there's a contact email.

```
Subject: Following up — [Role Title] Application

Hi [Name / Hiring Team],

I applied for the [Role Title] position on [date applied] and wanted to check in on the status of my application. I remain very enthusiastic about the opportunity to join [Company], particularly [one specific thing from your Company Research notes].

Please let me know if you need anything further from me.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## Post-Interview Thank You

```
Subject: Thank you — [Role Title] Interview

Hi [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the [Role Title] role. I enjoyed learning more about [specific thing discussed], and it's reinforced how well the role aligns with my experience in [relevant skill/project].

Please let me know if there's anything else I can provide to support my application.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```
